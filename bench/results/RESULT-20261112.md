# Bench result 2026-11-12 - R5 vs R4 vs OpenWrt main (serial + Tasmota cold cycles, PoE camera on lan7)

Rig: `bench/bench.py` + `bench/run-plan.sh` on the bench PC, console on the FTDI adapter, switch mains from Tasmota plug 1,
a PoE camera (1.6 W) on lan7. Each run = Tasmota off, 10 s, on; serial boot capture; 40 s settle; health check.
A run passes only if the console came up, the check ran, the camera port was delivering (R5/R4: `ubus`; main: `ethtool --show-pse`),
no PoE counter was above 0 and no `request-bad-checksum` / `Aborting request` / `giving up` / UART `oe:` count appeared on the console or in the report.

| Variant | Image / daemon | Cold boots | Pass | PoE log errors | Console after power-on | Console-flood test |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R5 plain | 25.12.6 + stock realtek-poe `e11ca9a3...` | 10 | 10 | 0 | 34.3 s avg | PASS (daemon recovered, ports unchanged) |
| R4 with-r4 | 25.12.6 + patched realtek-poe `ca4a119c...` | 10 | 10 | 0 | 34.2 s avg | PASS |
| main | OpenWrt main r0-ada52cc, kernel 6.18.60, kernel PSE driver, sysupgrade `4ba43a3c...` | 10 | 10 | 0 | 31.3 s avg | by hand only (see below): control 0 failures, flood ~80% "Bad message"; raw output NOT saved |

Main details: the driver found `ST32F100 MCU, BCM59111 (id 0xe111), 8 ports`; RAM boot first (no flash write) showed 8 of 8 ports answering;
then flashed with config kept; camera port `delivering power`, 1600 mW, in 10 of 10 boots.

## What this does not show
- Load: one 1.6 W camera. Nothing heavier, no budget pressure, no several-port start-up.
- Main: every port is `disabled` after boot (no daemon applies `/etc/config/poe`); the check switches lan7 on by hand (`ethtool --set-pse lan7 c33-pse-admin-control enable`).
  A shippable main build needs something that enables ports at boot. Not designed or tested.
- Main under the console flood: run by hand on the unit, three repeats of 30 rounds (2 `prio` writes + 1 read of one port) with and without a
  300-message `/dev/kmsg` flood at console level 8: control 0 of 60 writes / 0 of 30 reads failed; flood 47-49 of 60 writes and 25 of 30 reads failed,
  every error `netlink error: Bad message`, port state intact afterwards. That output was printed in the session and **not saved to a file**; the
  script that automates it is `bench/checks/03-main-flood.sh` (verdict FAIL/PASS/INCONCLUSIVE), but it has not been run on the unit since it was written.
  The older files `main-flood.txt` and `main-flood-ssh.txt` on the bench PC are from earlier versions of the script (garbled console typing; a disable/enable method
  that always fails on this build) and do not support any claim. Treat the flood result as indicative until the script is re-run and its output kept.
- A first R5 pass was discarded: a stopped plan left its cold-run process alive and two controllers cycled the switch at once.
  The rig now takes a lock. Raw files of that attempt are kept on the bench PC under `results/tainted/`.

## State left behind
Unit on 25.12.6 with the with-r4 daemon (`ca4a119c...`), config kept (VLAN 10 management address), camera delivering, `loglevel=4`.

## Addendum: OpenWrt main WITHOUT `loglevel=4` (default console level 15)

Same main build with only the DTS bootarg changed to `earlycon` (image sha256 `63b35f0c...` sysupgrade; `printk` read back as `15 4 1 15`).
- 10 reboots over ssh (`bench.py cold --warm`; the Tasmota plug no longer powered the switch, so no mains cuts): 10/10 passed.
  Each boot printed its full kernel log on the serial console (about 21 KB per boot); the PSE driver probed at about 18 s
  ("ST32F100 MCU, BCM59111, 8 ports"), all 8 ports answered `ethtool --show-pse`, the camera port was delivering power,
  and neither the serial log nor `dmesg` held any PoE error line.
- A first attempt with `--warm` absent was invalid and discarded: the switch was not on the plug, nothing rebooted.
- Reading: at start-up the kernel driver coped with the unquieted console in these 10 boots. The hand-run flood test (a burst of 300 messages
  during PoE traffic) is what broke it, so `loglevel=4` was not needed for a clean start-up here; whether it is needed against bursts rests on the hand-run flood above (output not saved).

### Same image, real cold boots (plug back on the switch, later the same day)
10 Tasmota mains cuts (off 10 s, on) with the no-`loglevel` image, `bench.py cold --label noquiet` via `run-plan.sh noquiet`:
**10/10 passed.** Console back 32 s after power-on in every run; each boot's serial log about 20 KB (full kernel log at level 15);
driver probe at 18.2-18.3 s every time; 8 of 8 ports answer; camera port delivering power in every run; 0 PoE error lines in serial logs
or reports. The unit was then rolled back to 25.12.6 + with-r4 (config kept).
Not tested: other PoE loads, long soak, and the burst case on a saved record (above).
